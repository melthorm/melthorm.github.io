# About me

I originally chose computer science because I wanted to be employed -- what a truly `far-fetched` dream now (or not I suppose) -- and because I really love math. Somehow, someway, that original reason has morphed into something different. Understanding how computers -- and for that matter computer science adjacent things like AI -- work is absurdly, absurdly interesting. Your computer somehow has to... `work`. Isn't that truly insane? Your computer is capable of so much and it is just a fastidiously-made hunk of metal. Also, I think the community within computer science gets a bad rap. It is very good, top-notch, best place I've ever had the pleasure (nerd) of being in -- though that may also be due to my lack of age.

For me, originally, I wanted to pursue biology due to wanting to learn as much as possible about `phylogenetics` (shoutout to my mentor in high school Dr. Emma Goldberg!). Actually, much of what I did during high school and the earlier part of freshman year was in service of that desire. It certainly helps that there is a veritable gold mine of math underlying phylogenetics. That original motivator was called into question recently, and I thought I was more captured by another area of biology -- that being whatever area of biology protein mechanisms is a part of. Currently though, after a very riveting microbiology lecture -- that adjective should never be used by a sane man to describe that subject -- I have decided that it is still fundamentally `phylogenetics` that draws me in. I've gained just a modicum of understanding my own psyche from that experience. I have perhaps realized it is more of wanting to understand life. Jesus, that sounds very grand. I am not nearly that smart. Wait, like lowkey, I'm actually rather stupid. This whole section is written in kind of a Victorian voice so it sounds smart and pretentious, but I am certainly not one of those! (hint: I think it's smart)

I really like chess. I really like piano. I really like creative writing. Yep, oh, and the academic stuff, ig. Most of the posts here will probably either be about those four things (or it'll be me `crashing out` for some stupid reason of which there are many).
